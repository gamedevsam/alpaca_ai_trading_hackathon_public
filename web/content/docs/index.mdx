If you invest by studying other people — a fund manager whose letters you read, an
investor whose disclosures you follow, a friend whose judgment you rate — you already do
most of this work. You just do it in a spreadsheet that forgets where every number came
from, and in a memory that can't tell you whether the manager you copied still holds the
thing you bought.

**Stock Rankings turns that work into a system.** It keeps your holdings and the books of
the people you follow in one place, combines them into a plan on terms you set, and shows
you exactly where your real portfolio has drifted from it — along with the trades that
would close the gap.

The division of labor never changes: **the system does the work, you make the calls.**
Every result is a proposal you can accept, ignore, or edit. Nothing trades, moves, or
changes without you.

## What you get out of it

<Cards>
  <Card title="One honest view of everything you own">
    Broker sync, CSV imports and hand-entered positions all land in the same place, with every number stamped with how
    current it is. No more reconciling six accounts by squinting at browser tabs.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The managers you follow, as data">
    Record what they hold and when. See what changed between any two disclosures — including the exit they never
    mentioned.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A plan that's yours, not a black box">
    Weight each manager by how much you trust them, set the rules you always apply, and get a target portfolio where
    every line traces back to a decision you made.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An answer to 'why do I own this?'">
    Every position carries a recorded reason. When a reason disappears, you find out — instead of holding someone else's
    abandoned idea for two years.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## How the pieces fit

Four steps, in order. Each is useful on its own; together they're the product.

1. **Get your data in.** Sync a portfolio tracker, import a CSV, or type positions in by
   hand. → [Getting started](/docs/getting-started)
2. **Follow the people you learn from.** Record their books as
   [sources](/docs/concepts/sources), with dates and evidence attached.
   → [Sources & the Blend Workbench](/docs/sources-and-blend)
3. **Combine them into a plan.** Set a [trust weight](/docs/concepts/blend) per source and
   the rules you always want applied; get an explainable
   [target portfolio](/docs/concepts/target) out.
4. **Close the gap — or don't.** [Reconcile](/docs/concepts/reconcile) that target against
   a real account and get the buy/sell list. It's a proposal. You decide.

Two AI surfaces work across all of it: the [AI Council](/docs/ai-council) inside the app,
and [your own AI assistant](/docs/mcp-assistant) connected from wherever you already chat.
Both stop at proposals.
