The **[AI Council](/docs/concepts/ai-council)** is a panel of AI portfolio managers you
can consult about your portfolio. Each has a distinct investing philosophy — written down
as a persona you can read and edit, not hidden inside a prompt.

Available on the **Pro** and **Ultimate** tiers.

## Why a panel and not one chatbot

Ask a single AI what to do with your portfolio and you get a confident answer shaped by
however you happened to phrase the question. Ask three with genuinely different
philosophies — a growth-minded one, a patient value one, a quality-compounder one — and
you get the argument instead. Where they agree, where they don't, and why.

That argument is the product. A conclusion that survives three hostile readings is worth
considerably more than the first plausible answer.

## Running a session

From the **AI → AI Council** page:

1. **Write a prompt** — a question, an idea you're weighing, or pick a template like a
   monthly portfolio review.
2. **Choose what it's grounded on** — one account, a group of them, or everything.
3. **Convene.** The panel debates across multiple rounds until it converges on a
   satisfaction threshold or hits the round limit you set.
4. **Read the synthesis.** A synthesizer writes up the result as prioritized actions —
   _trim this, research that_ — each with its reasoning and a priority level.

The session records what it was grounded on and whether that data was fresh at the time,
so a recommendation built on stale holdings says so.

## Grounded in your actual book

Council sessions are injected with facts about what you really hold — positions, weights,
portfolio-level metrics — from your synced or imported data. It isn't reasoning about the
market in the abstract; it's reasoning about your book, at its current weights, with your
concentration.

## Your panel, your models

- **Personas are editable.** Each manager's philosophy is a document you can rewrite —
  adjust its risk tolerance, sharpen its style, or add a new persona. Every edit is
  versioned, so you can see what changed and roll it back.
- **You choose the provider.** The Council runs on whichever AI provider you configure.
  Provider connectivity is visible in **Admin & diagnostics**, including an opt-in deep
  check that round-trips a real request.
- **No provider? It still runs — and says so.** With nothing configured, the Council falls
  back to a deterministic stand-in that is clearly labeled simulated. You will never be
  shown a canned response dressed up as a live model.

## Where it stops

A Council recommendation is **a proposal**. There is no path from "the panel recommends
trimming this" to an order being placed — acting on it goes through you, and then through
the same [reconcile](/docs/concepts/reconcile) and approval loop as everything else.

The panel's job is to make you a better decision-maker, not to replace you as one.
