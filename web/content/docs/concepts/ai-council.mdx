**The AI Council is a panel of AI portfolio managers you can consult about your
portfolio.** Each one has its own investing philosophy — a growth-minded one, a patient
value one, a quality-compounder one — written down as an editable persona rather than
hidden in a prompt.

You can talk to one of them, or convene the whole panel to debate a question and return a
synthesized recommendation. The Council is part of the Pro tier (see
[pricing](/pricing)).

## Why a panel instead of one assistant

Ask a single AI what to do with your portfolio and you get a confident answer shaped by
whatever framing you happened to use. Ask three with genuinely different philosophies and
you get the argument instead — where they agree, where they don't, and _why_. The
disagreement is the useful part; a consensus that survives it is worth more than a first
answer.

A session runs multiple rounds until the panel converges or hits your round limit, then a
synthesizer writes up the result: prioritized actions (trim this, research that), each
with a reason attached.

## Grounded in what you actually hold

Council sessions are injected with facts about your real book — positions, weights,
concentration — from your synced or imported data, and each run records which portfolio
it was grounded on and whether that data was fresh. It isn't reasoning about markets in
the abstract; it's reasoning about your book.

## Your panel, your models

- **Personas are editable.** Every manager's philosophy is a document you can rewrite —
  tune its risk tolerance, sharpen its style, or add a new persona entirely. Every edit is
  versioned, so you can see what changed and roll back.
- **You choose the model.** The Council runs on whichever AI provider you configure. If
  none is configured, it still runs — on a deterministic stand-in, clearly labeled as
  simulated, so you're never shown a canned answer dressed up as a live model.

## The hard limit

A Council recommendation is **a proposal, and nothing else.** There is no path from
"the panel recommends trimming this" to an order being placed. Acting on it runs through
you, and then through the same [reconcile](/docs/concepts/reconcile) and approval loop as
everything else here.
