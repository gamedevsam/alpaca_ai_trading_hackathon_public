Most "AI investing" tools give you one model, prompted once, answering in a vacuum. We
built something different: a **Council** — several AI portfolio managers, each with its
own persona and investing philosophy, that you can talk to individually or put in a
room together.

## Grounded, not generic

The single most important design decision behind the AI Council is that it never
reasons about the market in the abstract. Every conversation is grounded in **your
actual portfolio** — your real positions, weights, and performance — pulled straight
from your synced or imported holdings. Ask a portfolio manager what it thinks of your
allocation, and it's answering about _your_ allocation, not a hypothetical one.

## Two ways to use it

- **1:1 chat** — pick one advisor and talk through an idea, a position, or a question,
  the way you would with a single analyst.
- **Debate mode** — hand the whole Council a trade idea or a question and let them
  argue it out from their different perspectives before returning a synthesized
  recommendation. Disagreement is the point; it's how you find the weak spots in an
  idea before you act on it.

## You're the one who tunes it

Every advisor's system prompt — what we call its "SOUL" — is editable in the app. Don't
like how aggressively one advisor weighs momentum? Adjust it. Want a fourth advisor with
a completely different philosophy? Add one. Every edit is versioned so you can always
see exactly what changed and roll back if a tweak didn't land the way you expected.

The AI Council is part of the Pro and Ultimate plans, which activate at public launch —
