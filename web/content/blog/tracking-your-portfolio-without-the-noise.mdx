Before any AI advice or automated trading is useful, you need one thing to actually be
true: an accurate, trustworthy picture of what you hold. That's the foundation
everything else in Stock Rankings is built on.

## Three ways in, one model out

You can get a portfolio into Stock Rankings however fits your situation:

- **Sync it live** from your portfolio tracker, and it stays current on a schedule with
  no manual upkeep.
- **Enter it by hand** — useful for a small account or one you're modeling out.
- **Import a CSV** from your broker. We show you a diff before anything is saved, so a
  re-import updates your holdings in place instead of quietly duplicating them.

All three converge on the same underlying portfolio model, so nothing downstream — target
allocations, drift, the rebalance engine, performance history — cares how a position got
there.

## Know when you've drifted, and what to do about it

Set target allocations once, and Stock Rankings shows your actual drift from target at a
glance, plus a concrete trade plan to get back in line — not just a "you're overweight
tech" observation, but the actual buys and sells that would close the gap.

## Performance you can trust over time

Nightly valuation snapshots build a real time-weighted return series, so "how am I
doing" is answered with an actual performance history, not a single point-in-time
balance that resets your sense of progress every time you check in.

## Accurate data is what makes proposals trustworthy

None of this is tracking for tracking's sake. An accurate, freshness-aware picture of
what you hold is what lets everything above it — the blended target, the drift
analysis, the AI Council, your connected assistant — produce proposals you can actually
act on. Garbage in, confident-sounding garbage out; that's the failure mode this
foundation exists to prevent.
